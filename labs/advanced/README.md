## 🔥 Advanced Labs

Full scenarios that mirror what practitioners actually do.

</div>

---

This is where it gets serious. Advanced labs assume you're comfortable across offense and defense and ready for scenarios that look like real work. Expect to put in real time here — and to learn a lot.

Labs in this section focus on:

- **Red-team scenarios** — engagement-style exercises in your lab, from initial access through objectives.
- **Detection engineering** — designing, writing, and tuning detections so defenders catch the techniques you've practiced.
- **Sandboxed malware analysis** — safely examining malicious samples in an isolated environment to understand behavior and build defenses.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Everything here stays inside an isolated, self-contained lab. Handle samples with care and never run them outside a sandbox you control.

> [!NOTE]
> These are the labs we're most excited to build out, and the best place to share deep expertise. Got a red-team scenario, a detection you've engineered, or a safe malware-analysis walkthrough? Bring it to the e-board. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md): proposal first, code second.
