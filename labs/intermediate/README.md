## ⚙️ Intermediate Labs

Where individual skills become realistic workflows.

</div>

---

Ready to go deeper? Intermediate labs connect single techniques into workflows that play out over several steps and across both sides of the fight. Instead of running one tool, you'll chain them together and start reasoning about how an attack unfolds and how a defender reconstructs it.

Labs in this section focus on:

- **Multi-step attack and defense chains** — stringing together reconnaissance, exploitation, and follow-on actions, and defending against each stage.
- **Log analysis** — reading system, network, and application logs to piece together what actually happened.
- **Basic SIEM work** — pulling events into one place and writing queries and dashboards that surface what matters.

> [!NOTE]
> This section is just getting started, and we'd love your help filling it in. Worked through something that fits — a detection chain, a log-analysis walkthrough, a first SIEM setup? Propose it to the e-board and we'll help you turn it into a lab for everyone. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md): proposal first, code second.
